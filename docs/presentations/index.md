| 2026-07-14 | [The Future of Transcriptomics in an Agentic World](crick-rna-ideathon-2026/) | Manuel Corpas's opening for the Londonomics RNA Ideathon at the Francis Crick Institute (14 July 2026). Fourteen slides arguing that the future of transcriptomics is not a smarter model but a trusted operating layer: skills that carry a versioned context contract, fail safely, and keep a chain of custody. Reproduces the published Klim and Melamed TDP-43 knockdown result (STMN2 collapse, GSE121569) through the ClawBio `rnaseq-de` skill, then a nine-model, five-provider invariance grid where skill execution collapses model-to-model variation, with the same collapse shown in DNA from the pharmacogenomics benchmark. Grounded in two Cell Genomics papers (the Agentic Genomics workflow perspective, live 21 July, and the trustworthy skill-library benchmark in revision) and live ClawBio traction (94 skills, 3,700+ tests, 43 contributors, 1,000+ stars). Closes on the thesis that the community should build the shared component every trusted RNA agent uses, plus a build-with-us slide (book + community). An appendix slide gives the five verbatim benchmark requests. |
| 2026-06-24 | [Agentic Genomics in Practice (ClawBio × Genomic Intelligence)](agentic-genomics-gi-webinar-2026/) | Manuel Corpas's opening for the ClawBio × Genomic Intelligence webinar (24 June 2026, Zoom, 230+ registered). Fifteen-slide framing of the state of the art in agentic genomics before the Genomic Intelligence live model demos: the two waves of LLMs in biology, a four-condition definition of agentic genomics, the thesis that the bottleneck moves from producing analyses to validating them, and the validation bottleneck of silent plausible failure, including the real, publicly caught-and-fixed empty-file bug (a clean "all normal" report dosing 51 drugs). Grounded in the pharmacogenomics benchmark (44,550 scored evaluations; 80.6% free prompt &rarr; 95.5% skill reasoning &rarr; 100% skill execution), live ClawBio traction (~1k stars / 90 skills / 48 contributors), the counterintuitive result that the right guideline as free context made the model more dangerous, the ancestry accuracy gradient (72% European / 51% Latin American / 40% East African), and a three-tier validation framework (research / benchmarked / clinical). Closes on the conversational.clawbio.ai live demo and a Build-with-us slide (book Amazon QR + WhatsApp community). |
